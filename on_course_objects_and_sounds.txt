ON-COURSE OBJECTS AND SOUNDS: what a player sees and hears in Galena!!! Pocket Edition right now
=====================================================================================
Current state only. Keep this file in step with index.html and courses/courses.json (see CLAUDE.md): whenever an object or a sound is added,
removed or retuned, or a condition or frequency changes, update it in the same change, and delete entries for things that are gone.
Frequencies and conditions come from the code (buildLife / updateLife, the SFX table, songTick, birdTick, fartSwing in index.html), not from
watching a full round.

HOW THIS FILE IS ORGANISED
  GLOBAL        what every course has (objects, then sounds), after a table of which course has what.
  then one section per course, in the order of the course picker: Woodbine Bend, Eagle Ridge South, Eagle Ridge North, Eagle Ridge The General,
  Fossil Trace, Stonehenge, Thunderhawk, Bear Slide, Legends at Memphis National, Chestnut Mountain Disc Golf, Adler Park Disc Golf.
  Each course section has OBJECTS (what only that course has, with how often and when) then SOUNDS (the same), then SCENERY AND DATA (trees,
  water, buildings, roads, paths). A line "also everything in GLOBAL" is always true; "NOT here" lists what a reader might expect and will not find.
  Wording used for frequency: "always" = every time; "per hole" = decided each time a hole loads; "per trip" = each time a vehicle sets off;
  "per round" = once per round of 18; "rare/very rare" are said with the number.

B. OBJECTS ON EVERY COURSE
--------------------------
Bird flocks (2, high up, crossing the sky) (not at Fossil Trace)
  - 11 gulls: first pass about 6-20 s into a hole.
  - 8 sparrows: first pass about 28-42 s into a hole.
  - Each returns 25-75 s after it passes, from a new direction. Most holes show at least one if you wait.
  - Can be hit in principle ("Birdie... literally!"), but they fly high. A hit bird plays the leaf-rustle sound (below).

Hot air balloon (brown, red, orange and white stripes, wicker basket, burner flame; about 18 m across) (not at Fossil Trace)
  - Drifts slowly downwind across the sky at 38-60 m up, taking about 80-130 s to cross, bobbing and turning gently.
  - Rare: per hole, a 60% chance one is scheduled 10-65 s after arriving, 40% none at first; after one leaves, the next is due 100-220 s later.
  - Cannot be hit: the ball passes through it. Silent.

Sky and weather
  - Clouds drift across the sky and thicken as the weather changes.
  - Weather follows the clock, per course: the same for every player on a course at the same moment (solo, practice, multiplayer), so restarting changes nothing. Spells last about 1-2 hours
    and move in fronts (dry, fine, damp, rain, damp, fine); about 30% dry, 35% fine, 20% damp, 15% rain. A hole takes the weather of the moment it starts. Rain has falling drops and a darker sky. No rain sound (birdsong is only rarer in rain).
  - Rainbow: a faint one in light rain when the sky is not too cloudy (as a spell builds or eases off); it sits low in the east and has its own jingle (see sounds).
  - Wind: the flag flutters and a wind arrow shows on screen. Wind changes every hole.

Ground-level scenery
  - Grass blades and tufts around the camera; in the deep rough, tan and amber fescue (Woodbine only) or dark green long rough (every other golf course).
  - Wildflowers: small daisies in drifts (white, yellow, pink, lilac, orange, pale blue).
  - Tall reeds round ponds, creeks and ditches.
  - Trees from the lidar data; they block the ball. Broadleaf: the bigger half oaks (broad, deep green), the smaller half maples (compact, livelier
    green). Conifer: the bigger half northern pines (tall bare trunk, tufted crown), the smaller half blue Norwegian spruces (blue-green cones).
    Shrubs under 4 m.

Man-made objects
  - Flagstick, flag and cup on every green (on a disc golf course a basket instead).
  - Tee markers in four colours (white, yellow, blue, red), two per tee set, always standing inside the tee box. None on a disc golf course.
  - White boundary stakes (out of bounds); red stakes round the ponds (penalty areas).
  - Distance posts at 200, 150 and 100 m.
  - Buildings from OpenStreetMap (houses, farm buildings, sheds) within about 240 m of each hole; the ball can hit them ("Off the clubhouse wall!" style note and the tree-hit crack).
  - Cart paths, where the course's data has them (dark asphalt, 2.4 m wide). A hard surface: a ball bounces on it several times (restitution .8: a wedge shot
    hops 2 m or more, a drive 1 m, 7-9 hops before it rolls) and rolls fast. See the table for which courses have them.
  - Cart-path bridges (Eagle Ridge only): a ball that lands on a deck is on a cart path.
  - Roads out of bounds, for looks only (the ball does not touch them).

Practice areas
  - The practice green (four cups and flags, a close map) and the driving range (four flags, six hitting spots) are Woodbine Bend's whichever course is picked.
    They have all the GLOBAL sounds below and nothing course-specific; the practice green plays the cup sound on every holed putt.

Putting
  - On every golf course and the practice green: faint dark contour lines on the green with pure white to reddish pink dots flowing downhill (steeper is faster and pinker), and a chip saying how much uphill or downhill it is and how much and which way the putt breaks. The last putt's path stays on the green as a gold line until the next stroke.

C. SOUNDS ON EVERY COURSE
-------------------------
All of them are silenced by the Sound switch (Settings). Hole songs and the golf cart radio are also scaled by Song volume (Off / Low .125 / Med .35 / High 1).
Nothing plays on the title screen except the interface blip.

Shot sounds (a golf course)
  - Club strike, every full shot, at impact, with a short phone vibration. A recording per club type:
      driver (club 1)  sounds/driver.mp3      woods (3 wood, 5 wood...)  sounds/wood.mp3      wedges  sounds/wedge.mp3
      irons and hybrids  sounds/iron.mp3      (the putter: below)
    A mishit (quality below 1) uses the same recording slightly lower and softer and duller. If a file failed to load a synthesised click stands in.
  - Putt: a soft tap, sounds/putter.mp3, a little louder for a harder stroke (synthesised thud and click if the file is missing). Every putt.
  - Ball landing or bouncing: a short soft thud (synthesised), louder the faster the ball arrives; every landing and every bounce of the flight (including the
    hops on a cart path); not played when a shot is skipped.
Shot sounds (a disc golf course: CMDG and ADGC)
  - Release: a soft whoosh (synthesised) at every throw instead of a strike or tap.
  - Catch in the chains: a bright shower of metallic ticks (synthesised) when a disc is caught, with a short phone vibration.
  - Graze: a light version of the chain shower when a disc brushes the chains. Spit-out: a half chain shower and a clank, "Through the chains · spit out".
  - Cage / tray / band / pole: a dull steel clank, "Off the cage!" or "Clanked the basket".
Hitting things
  - Tree branches the ball passes through: a leaf rustle, "Through the branches".
  - A tree trunk: a dull thunk, "Off the trunk!".
  - A tree crown or building wall: a short crack, "Timber! Clipped the trees" (or the building's own note).
  - Water: a splash (noise burst), each time the ball enters water; with a random splash note ("Fed the fish!" etc.).
  - Flagstick: a short metallic ping, "Off the flagstick!" / "...and in!". Cup rim and lip-out: no sound (a lip-out only shows "Lip out · too firm").
  - Holed on a golf course: the recorded cup sound (sounds/cup.mp3) as the ball drops, then the "In the hole!" card (no chime)
    ("In the chains!" on a disc course; no cup recording there, the chain shower is the sound). If the shot is skipped the cup sound still plays.
  - Putting green practice: the cup sound on every holed putt (no chime).
  - Record cards (New personal / world record, Record beaten): the same four-note chime with confetti.
  - A ball that hits an animal, the plane or a cart: see each course (rustle for deer and birds, a yelp for a coyote, a moo for a cow, gobble for a turkey, squeak for a gopher, squirrel or the
    hot dog, thunk for the plane, the paraglider and a golf cart).
Interface
  - A short 700 Hz blip on button presses: Play, Practice, Settings, opening a sheet, changing club or aim range, changing course, the Play Hole tap that ends
    the fly-over, the mulligan ask and the mulligan, skipping a shot's replay, and the next-hole button.
Ambient
  - Birdsong (5 recordings, sounds/bird1-5.mp3, 7-11 s each, picked at random, never the same twice running): on every hole that has no song of its own
    (everywhere except Woodbine hole 7). The first one starts 1.5-4 s after the hole loads; after each, the next comes 4-16 s after it ends (26-38 s while it
    rains, and 40% quieter). Very quiet (level baked in), panned to its own side. Silent on the title screen, with the Sound switch off or the page hidden.
    Until the files have loaded (or offline, if not cached) there is no birdsong.
  - Rainbow jingle (synthesised): a rising run of bell tones ending in a held sparkle, about 2 s. Plays once each time a rainbow appears, only once it
    is well faded in and only while the camera is facing it (it sits low in the east).
  - Heard, not seen: one of 19 recordings (sounds/fart1-19.mp3, picked at random). At least once per round: the round is dealt one of its 18 holes where it is
    certain and every other hole has a 1 in 18 chance (practice: 1 in 18 per hole, no dealt hole). It plays only during a swing, the moment you start the backswing,
    on the 1st, 2nd or 3rd swing of that hole (picked at random; putts count). A dealt hole that ends first is dealt again later in the round. Loudness is the same as
    the hole-7 song at Med whatever the Song setting; only the Sound switch silences it. Much rarer than the balloon.
  - The synthesised background music engine (guitar and piano) is switched off: no course uses it.

D. NOT IN THE GAME (switched off or never built)
------------------------------------------------
  - The hole-info tee signs (Nr., par, yards): switched off (code kept; TEE_SIGNS_ON in index.html). Only signs listed in a course's signs.txt are shown.
  - The paraglider (PARAGLIDER_ON), the six small ground birds (SMALL_BIRDS_ON) and the four circling gulls (CIRCLING_GULLS_ON) are switched off.
  - No rabbits, butterflies or fish. Benches, huts and similar props exist in the engine from removed courses but no course uses them.
  - No rain sound, wind sound, crowd, footsteps, flag rustle or clubhouse sounds.
  - No horn or truck sound anywhere except Woodbine Bend (`scene.truckHorn`): the Eagle Ridge courses have no trucks, and Stonehenge's truck is silent.


#####################################################################################################
WOODBINE BEND (woodbine)
#####################################################################################################
Also everything in GLOBAL. Golden fescue deep rough. Par 72, gold tees 6,223 yd. Its practice green and range are the game's.

OBJECTS
-------
Wild turkeys (`scene.turkeys` = .4: a flock of four, one tom with a fanned tail and three hens, on about 4 holes in 10, decided per hole; the other holes have none)
  - First appear 4-24 s after arriving on a hole. Each walks across the line of play, stopping to peck, leaves the far side, and returns 14-54 s later.
  - On a hole that has the flock expect one or two at a time.
  - They bolt (run for 4-6 s) if the ball lands within about 16 m or flies within about 32 m.
  - Can be hit: "Clipped a turkey!", the ball ricochets.

Gopher, the groundhog (`scene.groundhog`; Caddyshack-style: brown, buck teeth, pops out of a dirt mound with a dark hole)
  - Appears only while the golfer stands over a full (non-putt) shot: 20-35 yd down the aim line (within a 15-degree cone, 7.5 either side) on fairway or rough, and
    pops up about 1 s after its mound fades in. If the spot is unsuitable it retries 2 s later.
  - Stays up 9-14 s, turning its head and watching the golfer, then ducks back down.
  - Rare: per hole, a 20% chance one is scheduled 10-65 s after arriving, 80% none at first; after one leaves, the next is due 100-220 s later.
  - Ducks instantly if the ball lands close by (within about 12 m, or flies within about 26 m).
  - Can be hit while up: "A menace to the golfing industry...".

Banner plane (`scene.bannerPlane`; white and red prop plane, low flyby)
  - Tows a rippling banner behind the tail reading "Hot tubbbbbb!!!" from both sides (text PLANE_BANNER in index.html). The ball passes through the banner and rope; only the plane can be hit.
  - Only appears right after a tee shot; sometimes crosses straight through the ball's path.
  - Each round is dealt 0, 1 or 2 planes (2/7 none, 3/7 one, 2/7 two; about 1.1 on average), spread over the holes left.
  - Can be hit: "Hit the plane!"

Dancing hot dog (`scene.hotdogHole` = 10; a giant, 10 ft: red sausage in a bun with a mustard zigzag, a smiling face, stick arms with white gloves, red sneakers)
  - Always on hole 10 (HOTDOG_P = 1), never on other holes. From the moment the hole loads: about 100 yd down the line from the tee on the fairway (the nearest fairway spot if that one is rough or a bunker).
  - Dances to a 100 bpm beat: bounces, sways, waves both arms, kicks alternate legs, sidesteps slowly across the fairway (never into the rough), always facing the golfer.
  - Can be hit (it bounces the ball off and spins): "Revenge of The Choker!".

Fountain (`scene.fountainHole` = 1): a small spray in the middle of the pond right of the first tee, about 10 ft high, always running. The ball does not hit it. Silent.

Moving golf cart (`scene.cartRide`; THE RAREST THING ON THE COURSE; same model as the parked ones, with two men: dark hair, grey trousers, the driver in a light blue shirt and cap, the passenger in a salmon shirt and cap)
  - Each par 4 or par 5 hole has a 2.84% chance (CARTRIDE_P), and only if the hole has a cart path on both sides of the line of play: holes 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 16, 17 and 18
    (holes 1, 4 and 9 have no route). A bit over one round in four sees it (1 - (1 - .0284)^11 = 27%). Not on the putting green or the range. `?cart` on the page address forces it (testing).
  - Sets off the moment the golfer presses Play Hole: appears on the cart path about 14 m behind the tee box (on holes 8 and 16 no path reaches the tee, so it starts 117 m and 197 m out),
    drives down the path at 22.5 mph, crosses the rough and fairway about 262 yd (240 m) from the tee (shorter holes: 62% of the way; it shifts up to 36 m to find a clear crossing) and parks on the
    path on the far side, where it stays. Wheels turn, it follows the ground, and a ball can hit it ("Off a golf cart!").

Highway and traffic (US 20; the stretch drawn is about 2.6 km, past the north side of the course and then east along Stockton's edge)
  - Two lanes, double yellow centre line, white edge lines, gravel shoulders; 9.7 m across; trees kept clear; out of bounds. Speed 55 mph, 35 mph on the town stretch (the east end).
  - Traffic keeps right, in either direction chosen at random per trip, and drives the whole stretch end to end (about two minutes at 55 mph). A vehicle never drives through the one in front. The ball passes through traffic.
  - 18-wheeler (red tractor with sleeper cab and roof deflector, 16 m white trailer with a red band; about 21 m): one truck. First sets off 8-33 s after arriving on a hole, then 20-60 s after each trip ends. 88-96% of the limit.
  - Subaru Legacy (blue-grey sedan): two. First sets off 2-10 s after arriving, the second 10-26 s; each again 6-25 s after a trip ends. 95-105% of the limit.
  - Hyundai Santa Fe (black SUV, 2002): two. First 4-14 s, second 12-28 s; same timing after a trip and same speed.

Side roads (out of bounds, looks only; no traffic): East Center Road along the south and South Brown Road (then Chicago Street into Stockton) along the east: two-lane chip-sealed, gravel shoulders, no centre line. Both run out to the horizon and end on US 20. No trees stand on them.

Buildings and props (all always there)
  - The clubhouse (`scene.clubhouse`): the long building by the parking lot; rust-brown vertical-board walls, green-framed windows, grey-green shingle roof, a gabled entrance with two limestone columns, a brick-paver porch, a round "Woodbine Bend" sign in the gable, shrubs along the front. Solid.
  - Double washroom near hole 18 (MEN and WOMEN doors). Two blue porta-potties. All solid.
  - The clubhouse parking lot (between holes 10 and 13): painted stalls and 8 parked cars that are always the same ones (sedans and SUVs in assorted colours, the Subaru and the Hyundai among them). Seen on holes 1, 7-10, 12, 13 and 16-18. The cars are solid; the lot is only paint.
  - Two empty golf carts (Club Car Precedent: black roof, off-white body, black bumpers and tyres, tan bench, two golf bags) parked nose to tail on the cart path north-east of the practice green, facing up the path toward the first hole (`scene.parkedCarts`). Solid ("Off a golf cart!").
  - The big Woodbine Bend entrance sign (green board on posts, a golf ball on a red tee at each end, stone-edged flower bed) beside Route 20 on the west side, facing the course; only seen from the holes nearest it.
  - The pump house: a small plain metal building by the 8th tee, no doors; solid.
  - Hole 7 sign ("Andy Lewis / Hole in One / 9/10/2026", text from signs.txt), with its own fly-over intro.

Practice green (southeast of the 1st tee, beside the parking lot): four cups and flags numbered 1-4, a collar, tall fescue, three large broadleaf trees round it; visible from hole 1.
Driving range (the field south of the cart road, west of the clubhouse lot): mown striped grass, two sand bunkers, a small target green, tall fescue along the west edge and the creek, large broadleaf trees along the creek, a long grey metal shed beside the lot (solid), white boundary stakes, four red flags on tall poles (no cup) and six hitting spots (two in bunkers). After each shot the camera rests on where the ball stopped for about a second, then returns.

SOUNDS
------
  - Hole 7 song (`scene.songs` 7: sounds/hole7-q.mp3, level 1): always on hole 7. Starts 3 s after you tap Play Hole (after the fly-over), fades in over 2.5 s, loops, fades out when you move to the next hole or leave. Scaled by Song volume (Off silent, Low .125, Med .35, High 1). No birdsong on hole 7 at all (the song replaces it).
  - Truck horn or engine rumble (a recording, picked at random: sounds/truckhorn.mp3 air horn or sounds/truckrumble.mp3 engine): about one truck trip in three (35%) has one, at a random point 25-75% of the way along the road, and it plays only if the truck is within about 650 m of the camera (level up to .3, falling off with distance). Not on trips without one.
  - Golf cart radio, "Around the World" (sounds/cartmusic.mp3), only when the moving cart appears (2.84% per par 4/5 hole with a path both sides, see Objects): starts when it sets off and plays from the cart until the next hole, looping. Loud within 20 m, fading with distance to nothing by 280 m, duller far away and panned to the side the cart is on. Scaled by Song volume; silent at Off.
  - Banner plane drone: a recording of a small plane (sounds/plane.mp3, about 6 s) once per flyby, when the plane starts its pass and the camera is within 260 m.
  - Ball hits: gobble (a turkey: two phrases of warbling chirps, synthesised), squeak (gopher or hot dog), thunk (the plane, a golf cart).
  - Plus all of GLOBAL: strikes, landings, trees, water, cup, interface blips, birdsong (not on hole 7), rainbow jingle, the rare unseen sound.

SCENERY AND DATA
----------------
  - Cart paths: about 7.4 km, found on the aerial and corrected by hand; they cross the course between holes, past the clubhouse and to the tees.
  - Trees: about 1,350 (800 broadleaf, 310 conifer, 240 shrubs) over the 1.4 km square round the course: lidar, hand-painted ones, and a pass against the July 2023 aerial.
  - Water: a creek, two ponds and 31 OSM bunkers on the course.
  - Buildings: OSM houses and farm buildings round the course, plus the props above.


#####################################################################################################
EAGLE RIDGE SOUTH (eagle_south)
#####################################################################################################
Also everything in GLOBAL. Dark green long rough (engine `longRough`) instead of fescue. OSM and lidar data only; nothing painted. Gold tees 6,361 yd, par 72.

OBJECTS
-------
Wild turkeys: the same flock and behaviour as Woodbine (`scene.turkeys` = .4: four birds on about 4 holes in 10, per hole).
Gopher (`scene.groundhog`): as at Woodbine (20% per hole, up 9-14 s, only over a full shot).
Banner plane (`scene.bannerPlane`): as at Woodbine ("Hot tubbbbbb!!!", right after a tee shot, 0-2 per round).

Traffic on the roads (`scene.traffic`): occasional cars, ONLY the blue-grey Subaru Legacy and the black Hyundai Santa Fe: one of each. No truck, ever.
  - The Subaru first sets off 25-65 s after arriving on a hole, the Hyundai 60-120 s; each again 45-135 s after a trip ends.
  - 28 mph, right-hand lane, on one of the roads in view (the longer and nearer the tee, the likelier; roads under 80 m are not used), end to end of the piece in view. Per trip, not per hole. Cannot be hit.

Roads (out of bounds, for looks only): every OSM road, drive and service road within 230 m of a hole (42 pieces: Eagle Ridge Drive and the housing streets beside the course), each drawn 7.2 m wide, chip-sealed with gravel shoulders, no centre line, on the hole and on the hole map. Only what could be seen is drawn: the parts within about 320 m of the line from the tee to the cup, inside the hole's terrain. No trees stand on them.

Cart paths: OSM's cart-path lines (about 8.5 km, 2.4 m wide; nobody painted them). One continuous network: where a path meets a road the two ends are joined and the path is drawn over the road; five hand-closed gaps (by the 18th green and between holes 13 and 16); the creek crossings are carried by the bridges.

Cart-path bridges (ten; `CART_BRIDGES`): holes 3, 8 (two: from the tee box towards the fairway, and by the green), 9, 10 (two: the second path three-quarters of the way down, and by the green), 13, 14, between the 14th green and the 15th tee, and 18. A grey concrete deck 2.6 m wide and 9-21 m long (as long as the gap OSM leaves in the path), level with the higher bank and clear of the water, girders under it, a sill at each end, timber rails and posts. A ball that lands on the deck is on a cart path (hard, bounces and rolls as on any cart path, flat, never water); the rails and girders are scenery the ball passes through. Silent.

Coyote (`scene.coyote` = .15: about 3 holes in 20 have one, and a quarter of those a pair): gray with a white belly, chest and muzzle, big upright ears, a bushy gray tail with a dark tip, about 0.6 m at the shoulder. It trots across the hole (2 m/s), stops to sniff with its nose down and look round, and bolts (9.5 m/s, tail streaming) when the ball lands or the golfer comes within about 20 m, or 50 m while the ball flies. Can be hit (the body, not the legs): "Wile E. would be proud!", the ball ricochets and it yelps and runs. Comes back 40-120 s after it leaves.
Cows (`scene.cows`: hole 3 only, on 50% of visits to it (`chance` .5), never anywhere else): three, one brown, one black and one Holstein (white with black patches), each drawn 1.2x a real dairy cow (about 1.8 m at the back, 2.8 m long; `COW_K`), standing out of bounds, just behind the white boundary stakes south-west of the green (about 36 m from the cup), spread over a patch about 10 m across. They are never on the course: every spot they stand on or walk to is out of bounds, and they only move between the trees there.
