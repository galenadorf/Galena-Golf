ON-COURSE OBJECTS: what a player can see in Galena!!! Golf right now
=====================================================================
Current state only. Keep this file in step with index.html (see CLAUDE.md).
Frequencies come from the code (buildLife / updateLife in index.html), not from watching a full round.

ANIMALS
-------
Wild turkeys (a flock of four on about 4 holes in 10: one tom with a fanned tail, three hens; the other holes have none)
  - First appear 4-24 s after arriving on a hole.
  - Each walks across the line of play, stopping to peck, leaves the far side, and returns 14-54 s later.
  - On a hole that has the flock, expect one or two at a time.
  - They bolt (run for 4-6 s) if the ball lands within ~16 m or flies within ~32 m.
  - Can be hit: "Clipped a turkey!", gobble sound, the ball ricochets.

Bird flocks (2, high up, crossing the sky)
  - 11 gulls: first pass ~6-20 s into a hole.
  - 8 sparrows: first pass ~28-42 s into a hole.
  - Each returns 25-75 s after it passes, from a new direction. Most holes show at least one if you wait.
  - Can be hit in principle ("Birdie... literally!"), but they fly high.

AIRCRAFT
--------
Small plane (white and red prop plane, low flyby)
  - Tows a rippling banner on a rope (its length follows the text: letters a fixed 1.5 m tall) behind the tail, reading "Hot tubbbbbb!!!" from both sides (text is PLANE_BANNER in index.html).
  - The ball passes through the banner and rope; only the plane itself can be hit.
  - Only appears right after a tee shot; sometimes crosses straight through the ball's path.
  - Each round is dealt 0, 1 or 2 planes (2/7 none, 3/7 one, 2/7 two; ~1.1 average), spread over the holes left.
  - Can be hit: "Hit the plane!"

Hot air balloon (brown, red, orange and white stripes, wicker basket, burner flame; ~18 m across)
  - Drifts slowly downwind across the sky at 38-60 m up, taking ~80-130 s to cross, bobbing and turning gently.
  - Rare: per hole, 60% chance one is scheduled 10-65 s after arriving, 40% none at first;
    after one leaves, the next is due 100-220 s later.
  - Cannot be hit: the ball passes through it.

Gopher (Caddyshack-style: brown, buck teeth, pops out of a dirt mound with a dark hole)
  - Appears only while the golfer stands over a full (non-putt) shot: 20-35 yd down the aim line (within a 15-degree wide cone, 7.5 either side) on
    fairway or rough, and pops up ~1 s after its mound fades in. If the spot is unsuitable it retries 2 s later.
  - Stays up 9-14 s, turning its head and watching the golfer, then ducks back down.
  - Rare: per hole, 20% chance one is scheduled 10-65 s after arriving, 80% none at first;
    after one leaves, the next is due 100-220 s later.
  - Ducks instantly if the ball lands close by (within ~12 m, or flies within ~26 m).
  - Can be hit while up: "A menace to the golfing industry...", with a squeak.

Dancing hot dog (hole 10 only; a giant, 10 ft: red sausage in a bun with a mustard zigzag, a smiling face, stick arms with white gloves, red sneakers)
  - Always on hole 10 (`HOTDOG_P` = 1), never on other holes. From the moment the hole loads: about 100 yd down the line from the tee, on the fairway (it picks the nearest fairway spot if the
    exact one is rough or a bunker).
  - Dances to a 100 bpm beat: bounces, sways, waves both arms, kicks alternate legs, and sidesteps slowly back and forth across the fairway (never into the
    rough), always facing the golfer.
  - Can be hit (it bounces the ball off and spins): "Revenge of The Choker!", with a squeak.

HIGHWAY AND TRAFFIC
-------------------
US 20 (two lanes, double yellow centre line, white edge lines, gravel shoulders; 9.7 m across)
  - Runs past the north side of the course, a few metres outside the boundary, then east along the edge of Stockton; the whole
    stretch inside the game's map (about 2.6 km) is drawn. Trees are kept clear of it. It is out of bounds.
  - Speed limit 55 mph, 35 mph on the town stretch (east end).
  - Traffic keeps to the right-hand lane, in either direction chosen at random, and drives the whole stretch end to end
    (about two minutes at 55 mph). A vehicle never drives through the one in front: it slows to follow.
  - Cannot be hit: the ball passes through the traffic.
18-wheeler (red tractor with sleeper cab and roof deflector, 16 m white trailer with a red band; ~21 m long)
  - One truck: first sets off 8-33 s after arriving on a hole, then 20-60 s after each trip ends. Drives at 88-96% of the limit.
  - Semi-rare truck sound (a recorded air horn or engine rumble, picked at random): about one trip in three has one, somewhere along
    the road, and it plays only if the truck is within ~650 m of the camera (louder when closer).
Car (blue-grey sedan, a 2016 Subaru Legacy)
  - Two Subarus: the first sets off 2-10 s after arriving, the second 10-26 s; each starts again 6-25 s after a trip ends.
    Drives at 95-105% of the limit.
Car (black SUV, a 2002 Hyundai Santa Fe)
  - Two of them, same timing and speed as the Subarus: first sets off 4-14 s after arriving, second 12-28 s.

SKY AND WEATHER
---------------
  - Clouds drift across the sky and thicken as the weather changes.
  - Weather spells last 6-9 holes: clear, damp, rain, damp, fine. Rain has falling drops and darker sky.
  - Rainbow: a faint one in light rain when the sky is not too cloudy (as a spell builds or eases off); plays a short jingle once each time it appears, but only once it is well faded in and the camera is facing it (it sits low in the east).
  - Wind: the flag flutters and a wind arrow shows on screen.

  - Heard, not seen: one of 8 short recordings (sounds/fart1-8.mp3, picked at random) plays at least once per round: the round is dealt one of its 18 holes where it is certain, and every
    other hole has a 1 in 18 chance (practice: 1 in 18 per hole); it plays only during a swing, the moment you start the backswing, on the 1st, 2nd or 3rd swing of that hole (picked at random, putts count; a dealt hole that ends first is dealt again, later in the round); much rarer than the balloon. Same loudness as the hole-7 song at its Med setting, whatever the Song setting; only the Sound switch silences it.

GROUND-LEVEL SCENERY
--------------------
  - Grass blades and tufts around the camera; tan and amber fescue in the deep rough.
  - Wildflowers: small daisies in drifts (white, yellow, pink, lilac, orange, pale blue).
  - Tall reeds round ponds, the creek and ditches.
  - Trees from the lidar data; they block the ball. Broadleaf: the bigger half oaks (broad, deep green), the smaller half maples (compact, livelier green). Conifer: the bigger half northern pines (tall bare trunk, tufted crown), the smaller half blue Norwegian spruces (blue-green cones). Shrubs as before.

MAN-MADE OBJECTS
----------------
  - Flagstick, flag and cup on every green.
  - Tee markers in four colours (white, yellow, blue, red), two per tee set, always standing inside the tee box (a narrow box gets a narrower gap).
  - White boundary stakes (out of bounds); red stakes round the ponds (penalty areas).
  - Distance posts at 200, 150 and 100 m.
  - The clubhouse (the long building by the parking lot, after photos of its front): rust-brown vertical-board walls, green-framed windows, a grey-green shingle roof, a gabled entrance with two limestone columns, a brick-paver porch and a round "Woodbine Bend" sign in the gable, shrubs along the front. Solid, so a ball hits it.
  - Buildings from OpenStreetMap (houses, farm buildings, sheds) within ~240 m of each hole; the ball can hit them.
  - Double washroom near hole 18 (MEN and WOMEN doors).
  - Two blue porta-potties (the ball can hit them).
  - The clubhouse parking lot (between holes 10 and 13): asphalt with painted stalls (violet area on site_mask.png, stalls laid out by site_paint.py) and 8 parked cars that are always the
    same ones: sedans and SUVs in assorted colours, among them the Subaru and the Hyundai. Seen on holes 1, 7-10, 12, 13 and 16-18. Cars are solid, so a ball hits them (the lot itself is only paint).
  - Two empty golf carts (Club Car Precedent: black roof on thin posts, off-white body, black bumpers and tyres, tan bench seat, two golf bags on the back) parked nose to
    tail on the cart path north-east of the practice green, facing up the path toward the first hole. Always there; solid, so a ball hits them ("Off a golf cart!").
    Positions are `CART_PROPS` in index.html, not painted.
  - THE RAREST THING ON THE COURSE: a moving golf cart (same model) with two men in it (dark hair, grey trousers; the driver in a light blue #C4DCF0 shirt and cap, the
    passenger in a salmon #F7A780 shirt and cap). Each par 4 or par 5 hole has a 2.84% chance of one (`CARTRIDE_P`), and only if the hole has a cart path on both sides of the
    line of play, which was holes 3, 5, 6, 10, 12, 13, 14, 16, 17 and 18: about one round in four sees it (1 - (1 - .0284)^10 = 25%), while the hot dog is on every round. It sets off
    the moment the golfer presses Play Hole (`cartRideGo`, from `beginHole`): it appears on the cart path point nearest a spot 14 m behind the tee box, by the back tees (found by a search along the path cells to the crossing point, so it follows the path round bends and over small gaps),
    drives down the path at 22.5 mph, crosses the rough and fairway at about 262 yd (240 m) from the tee (shorter holes: 62% of the way; it shifts up to 36 m along the hole to
    find a clear crossing) and parks on the path on the far side, lined up with it, where it stays. Wheels turn, it follows the ground, and a ball can hit it. The route
    is traced from the cart path surface by `cartRoute`. It has a radio: "Around the World" (`sounds/cartmusic.mp3`) starts when it sets off and plays from the cart (loud within 20 m, fading with distance to nothing by 280 m, duller and panned to the cart's side; scaled by the Song volume setting) until the next hole (`cartMusicTick`). Not in the putting green or the range. Add `?cart` to the page address to force it on every such hole (testing).
  - The big Woodbine Bend entrance sign (green board on posts, a golf ball on a red tee at each end, stone-edged flower bed) beside Route 20 on the west side of the course, facing in toward the course. Painted with paint_edit.py / paint_read.py; only seen from the holes nearest it.
  - The pump house: a small plain metal building (corrugated grey siding and roof, no doors) by the 8th tee, painted with paint_edit.py / paint_read.py; solid, so a ball hits it.
  - Hole 7 sign ("Andy Lewis / Hole in One / 9/10/2026", text from signs.txt), with its own fly-over intro.

Cart paths (about 7.4 km, 2.4 m wide, dark asphalt): found on the aerial and corrected by hand. A hard surface, a ball bounces on it several times (restitution .8, keeping its bounce at speed: a wedge shot hops 2 m or more, a drive 1 m, 7-9 hops before it rolls) and rolls fast. They cross the course between the holes, past the clubhouse and to the tees.

Side roads (out of bounds, for looks only): East Center Road along the south of the course and South Brown Road (then Chicago Street into Stockton) along the east; plain two-lane chip-sealed roads with gravel shoulders, no centre line, no traffic. Both run out to the horizon and end on US 20; no trees stand on them.

Practice putting green (southeast of the 1st tee, beside the parking lot): a green with four cups and flags (numbered 1-4), a collar, tall fescue joined to the course's own, and three large broadleaf trees round it. Visible from hole 1; played from Practice > Putting green.
Driving range (the field south of the cart road, west of the clubhouse lot): played from Practice > Driving range. Mown striped grass with two sand bunkers (the big one near the road, one beside a target green), a small target green with a collar, tall fescue along the west edge and the creek side, large broadleaf trees along the creek, a long grey metal shed (the same corrugated model as the pump house, solid) beside the lot, and white boundary stakes. Four red flags on tall poles (numbered 1-4, nearest first, no cup: the ball cannot be holed) to aim at, and six hitting spots (two are in bunkers); the ball is teed on grass spots. All four flags stay up; the Flag button picks which one the aim snaps to. After each shot the camera follows the ball, rests on where it stopped for about a second, then returns to the hitting spot.

Fountain (hole 1 only): a small spray in the middle of the pond right of the first tee, about 10 ft high, always running. The ball does not hit it.

NOT IN THE GAME
---------------
  - The hole-info tee signs (Nr., par, yards) are switched off (code kept; set TEE_SIGNS_ON = true in index.html). Only signs listed in signs.txt are shown.
  - The paraglider is switched off (code kept; set PARAGLIDER_ON = true in index.html to bring it back).
  - The six small birds (pigeons and crows on the ground and in treetops) and the four gulls circling over the hole are switched off (SMALL_BIRDS_ON and CIRCLING_GULLS_ON in index.html).
  - No deer, squirrels, rabbits, butterflies or fish (the gopher is the only burrowing animal). Nothing else drives, and there are no bridges (the creek has none). (The stats screen only jokes "fed the fish" for balls in the water.)
  - Benches, huts, golf carts and similar props exist in the engine from the removed courses but Woodbine does not use them.
