ON-COURSE OBJECTS: what a player can see in Galena!!! Golf right now
=====================================================================
Current state only. Keep this file in step with index.html and courses/courses.json (see CLAUDE.md).
Frequencies come from the code (buildLife / updateLife in index.html), not from watching a full round.
Part 1 is the same on every course. Part 2 has a section per course: what only that course has (its `scene` settings in
courses/courses.json, and what its painted data and OpenStreetMap give it). A new course adds its own section to part 2.


PART 1: ON EVERY COURSE
#######################

ANIMALS
-------
Bird flocks (2, high up, crossing the sky)
  - 11 gulls: first pass ~6-20 s into a hole.
  - 8 sparrows: first pass ~28-42 s into a hole.
  - Each returns 25-75 s after it passes, from a new direction. Most holes show at least one if you wait.
  - Can be hit in principle ("Birdie... literally!"), but they fly high.

AIRCRAFT
--------
Hot air balloon (brown, red, orange and white stripes, wicker basket, burner flame; ~18 m across)
  - Drifts slowly downwind across the sky at 38-60 m up, taking ~80-130 s to cross, bobbing and turning gently.
  - Rare: per hole, 60% chance one is scheduled 10-65 s after arriving, 40% none at first;
    after one leaves, the next is due 100-220 s later.
  - Cannot be hit: the ball passes through it.

HIGHWAY AND TRAFFIC (on a course whose data has a highway: `HIGHWAY` in its tools/course/courses/<key>/course.py)
-------------------
  - The road is two lanes, double yellow centre line, white edge lines, gravel shoulders; 9.7 m across. The whole stretch inside the
    game's map is drawn. Trees are kept clear of it. It is out of bounds.
  - Traffic keeps to the right-hand lane, in either direction chosen at random, and drives the whole stretch end to end
    (about two minutes at 55 mph). A vehicle never drives through the one in front: it slows to follow.
  - Cannot be hit: the ball passes through the traffic.
18-wheeler (red tractor with sleeper cab and roof deflector, 16 m white trailer with a red band; ~21 m long)
  - One truck: first sets off 8-33 s after arriving on a hole, then 20-60 s after each trip ends. Drives at 88-96% of the limit.
  - Semi-rare truck sound (a recorded air horn or engine rumble, picked at random): about one trip in three has one, somewhere along
    the road, and it plays only if the truck is within ~650 m of the camera (louder when closer).
Car (blue-grey sedan, a 2016 Subaru Legacy)
  - Two Subarus: the first sets off 2-10 s after arriving, the second 10-26 s; each starts again 6-25 s after a trip ends.
    Drives at 95-105% of the limit.
Car (black SUV, a 2002 Hyundai Santa Fe)
  - Two of them, same timing and speed as the Subarus: first sets off 4-14 s after arriving, second 12-28 s.

SKY AND WEATHER
---------------
  - Clouds drift across the sky and thicken as the weather changes.
  - Weather spells last 6-9 holes: clear, damp, rain, damp, fine. Rain has falling drops and darker sky.
  - Rainbow: a faint one in light rain when the sky is not too cloudy (as a spell builds or eases off); plays a short jingle once each time it appears, but only once it is well faded in and the camera is facing it (it sits low in the east).
  - Wind: the flag flutters and a wind arrow shows on screen.

  - Heard, not seen: one of 8 short recordings (sounds/fart1-8.mp3, picked at random) plays at least once per round: the round is dealt one of its 18 holes where it is certain, and every
    other hole has a 1 in 18 chance (practice: 1 in 18 per hole); it plays only during a swing, the moment you start the backswing, on the 1st, 2nd or 3rd swing of that hole (picked at random, putts count; a dealt hole that ends first is dealt again, later in the round); much rarer than the balloon. Same loudness as the hole-7 song at its Med setting, whatever the Song setting; only the Sound switch silences it.

GROUND-LEVEL SCENERY
--------------------
  - Grass blades and tufts around the camera; in the deep rough, tan and amber fescue or dark green long rough, per course (see part 2).
  - Wildflowers: small daisies in drifts (white, yellow, pink, lilac, orange, pale blue).
  - Tall reeds round ponds, the creek and ditches.
  - Trees from the lidar data; they block the ball. Broadleaf: the bigger half oaks (broad, deep green), the smaller half maples (compact, livelier green). Conifer: the bigger half northern pines (tall bare trunk, tufted crown), the smaller half blue Norwegian spruces (blue-green cones). Shrubs as before.

MAN-MADE OBJECTS
----------------
  - Flagstick, flag and cup on every green (on a disc golf course a basket instead: see CMDG in part 2).
  - Tee markers in four colours (white, yellow, blue, red), two per tee set, always standing inside the tee box (a narrow box gets a narrower gap). None on a disc golf course.
  - White boundary stakes (out of bounds); red stakes round the ponds (penalty areas).
  - Distance posts at 200, 150 and 100 m.
  - Buildings from OpenStreetMap (houses, farm buildings, sheds) within ~240 m of each hole; the ball can hit them.
  - Cart paths (where the course's data has them: dark asphalt, 2.4 m wide). A hard surface, a ball bounces on it several times (restitution .8, keeping its bounce at speed: a wedge shot hops 2 m or more, a drive 1 m, 7-9 hops before it rolls) and rolls fast.

PRACTICE AREAS
--------------
  - The practice green and the driving range are Woodbine Bend's (see part 2), whichever course is picked.

NOT IN THE GAME
---------------
  - The hole-info tee signs (Nr., par, yards) are switched off (code kept; set TEE_SIGNS_ON = true in index.html). Only signs listed in signs.txt are shown.
  - The paraglider is switched off (code kept; set PARAGLIDER_ON = true in index.html to bring it back).
  - The six small birds (pigeons and crows on the ground and in treetops) and the four gulls circling over the hole are switched off (SMALL_BIRDS_ON and CIRCLING_GULLS_ON in index.html).
  - No rabbits, butterflies or fish. Deer and squirrels only at Stonehenge and Thunderhawk; the gopher, the banner plane and wild turkeys only at Woodbine Bend and Eagle Ridge South and North. Nothing else drives, and there are no bridges (the creek has none). (The stats screen only jokes "fed the fish" for balls in the water.)
  - Benches, huts and similar props exist in the engine from the removed courses but no course uses them.


PART 2: COURSE BY COURSE
########################

WOODBINE BEND (woodbine)
========================

Wild turkeys (a flock of four on about 4 holes in 10, `scene.turkeys` = .4: one tom with a fanned tail, three hens; the other holes have none)
  - First appear 4-24 s after arriving on a hole.
  - Each walks across the line of play, stopping to peck, leaves the far side, and returns 14-54 s later.
  - On a hole that has the flock, expect one or two at a time.
  - They bolt (run for 4-6 s) if the ball lands within ~16 m or flies within ~32 m.
  - Can be hit: "Clipped a turkey!", gobble sound, the ball ricochets.

Gopher, the groundhog (`scene.groundhog`; Caddyshack-style: brown, buck teeth, pops out of a dirt mound with a dark hole)
  - Appears only while the golfer stands over a full (non-putt) shot: 20-35 yd down the aim line (within a 15-degree wide cone, 7.5 either side) on
    fairway or rough, and pops up ~1 s after its mound fades in. If the spot is unsuitable it retries 2 s later.
  - Stays up 9-14 s, turning its head and watching the golfer, then ducks back down.
  - Rare: per hole, 20% chance one is scheduled 10-65 s after arriving, 80% none at first;
    after one leaves, the next is due 100-220 s later.
  - Ducks instantly if the ball lands close by (within ~12 m, or flies within ~26 m).
  - Can be hit while up: "A menace to the golfing industry...", with a squeak.

Banner plane (`scene.bannerPlane`; white and red prop plane, low flyby)
  - Tows a rippling banner on a rope (its length follows the text: letters a fixed 1.5 m tall) behind the tail, reading "Hot tubbbbbb!!!" from both sides (text is PLANE_BANNER in index.html).
  - The ball passes through the banner and rope; only the plane itself can be hit.
  - Only appears right after a tee shot; sometimes crosses straight through the ball's path.
  - Each round is dealt 0, 1 or 2 planes (2/7 none, 3/7 one, 2/7 two; ~1.1 average), spread over the holes left.
  - Can be hit: "Hit the plane!"

Dancing hot dog (hole 10 only, `scene.hotdogHole`; a giant, 10 ft: red sausage in a bun with a mustard zigzag, a smiling face, stick arms with white gloves, red sneakers)
  - Always on hole 10 (`HOTDOG_P` = 1), never on other holes. From the moment the hole loads: about 100 yd down the line from the tee, on the fairway (it picks the nearest fairway spot if the
    exact one is rough or a bunker).
  - Dances to a 100 bpm beat: bounces, sways, waves both arms, kicks alternate legs, and sidesteps slowly back and forth across the fairway (never into the
    rough), always facing the golfer.
  - Can be hit (it bounces the ball off and spins): "Revenge of The Choker!", with a squeak.

US 20 (the highway, with the traffic in part 1)
  - Runs past the north side of the course, a few metres outside the boundary, then east along the edge of Stockton; the stretch drawn is about 2.6 km.
  - Speed limit 55 mph, 35 mph on the town stretch (east end).

Side roads (out of bounds, for looks only): East Center Road along the south of the course and South Brown Road (then Chicago Street into Stockton) along the east; plain two-lane chip-sealed roads with gravel shoulders, no centre line, no traffic. Both run out to the horizon and end on US 20; no trees stand on them.

Buildings and props
  - The clubhouse (`scene.clubhouse`: the long building by the parking lot, after photos of its front): rust-brown vertical-board walls, green-framed windows, a grey-green shingle roof, a gabled entrance with two limestone columns, a brick-paver porch and a round "Woodbine Bend" sign in the gable, shrubs along the front. Solid, so a ball hits it.
  - Double washroom near hole 18 (MEN and WOMEN doors).
  - Two blue porta-potties (the ball can hit them).
  - The clubhouse parking lot (between holes 10 and 13): asphalt with painted stalls (violet area on site_mask.png, stalls laid out by site_paint.py) and 8 parked cars that are always the
    same ones: sedans and SUVs in assorted colours, among them the Subaru and the Hyundai. Seen on holes 1, 7-10, 12, 13 and 16-18. Cars are solid, so a ball hits them (the lot itself is only paint).
  - Two empty golf carts (Club Car Precedent: black roof on thin posts, off-white body, black bumpers and tyres, tan bench seat, two golf bags on the back) parked nose to
    tail on the cart path north-east of the practice green, facing up the path toward the first hole. Always there; solid, so a ball hits them ("Off a golf cart!").
    Positions are `scene.parkedCarts` in courses/courses.json, not painted.
  - The big Woodbine Bend entrance sign (green board on posts, a golf ball on a red tee at each end, stone-edged flower bed) beside Route 20 on the west side of the course, facing in toward the course. Painted with paint_edit.py / paint_read.py; only seen from the holes nearest it.
  - The pump house: a small plain metal building (corrugated grey siding and roof, no doors) by the 8th tee, painted with paint_edit.py / paint_read.py; solid, so a ball hits it.
  - Hole 7 sign ("Andy Lewis / Hole in One / 9/10/2026", text from signs.txt), with its own fly-over intro.

THE RAREST THING ON THE COURSE: a moving golf cart (`scene.cartRide`; same model as the parked ones) with two men in it (dark hair, grey trousers; the driver in a light blue #C4DCF0 shirt and cap, the
    passenger in a salmon #F7A780 shirt and cap). Each par 4 or par 5 hole has a 2.84% chance of one (`CARTRIDE_P`), and only if the hole has a cart path on both sides of the
    line of play, which is holes 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 16, 17 and 18 (checked in the browser; holes 1, 4 and 9 have no route): a bit over one round in four sees it (1 - (1 - .0284)^11 = 27%), while the hot dog is on every round. It sets off
    the moment the golfer presses Play Hole (`cartRideGo`, from `beginHole`): it appears on the cart path point nearest a spot 14 m behind the tee box, by the back tees (found by a search along the path cells to the crossing point, so it follows the path round bends and over small gaps; on holes 8 and 16 no path reaches back to the tee, so it starts 117 m and 197 m out),
    drives down the path at 22.5 mph, crosses the rough and fairway at about 262 yd (240 m) from the tee (shorter holes: 62% of the way; it shifts up to 36 m along the hole to
    find a clear crossing) and parks on the path on the far side, lined up with it, where it stays. Wheels turn, it follows the ground, and a ball can hit it. The route
    is traced from the cart path surface by `cartRoute`. It has a radio: "Around the World" (`sounds/cartmusic.mp3`) starts when it sets off and plays from the cart (loud within 20 m, fading with distance to nothing by 280 m, duller and panned to the cart's side; scaled by the Song volume setting) until the next hole (`cartMusicTick`). Not in the putting green or the range. Add `?cart` to the page address to force it on every such hole (testing).

Cart paths: about 7.4 km, found on the aerial and corrected by hand. They cross the course between the holes, past the clubhouse and to the tees.

Trees: about 1,350 (800 broadleaf, 310 conifer, 240 shrubs) over the 1.4 km square round the course: the 2019 lidar trees, the ones painted by hand
    (trees_mask.png, the practice green and the range), and a pass against the July 2023 aerial (`trees_aerial.py`): about 400 added where the aerial
    shows canopy nobody had placed (40 of them inside the boundary; most along US 20, in the Stockton yards and along the creek), about 230 crowns
    widened to what the aerial shows, and 27 lidar "trees" taken out: on roofs, grain bins and gravel, and seven standing in the range's metal shed.

Fountain (hole 1 only, `scene.fountainHole`): a small spray in the middle of the pond right of the first tee, about 10 ft high, always running. The ball does not hit it.

Practice putting green (southeast of the 1st tee, beside the parking lot): a green with four cups and flags (numbered 1-4), a collar, tall fescue joined to the course's own, and three large broadleaf trees round it. Visible from hole 1; played from Practice > Putting green.
Driving range (the field south of the cart road, west of the clubhouse lot): played from Practice > Driving range. Mown striped grass with two sand bunkers (the big one near the road, one beside a target green), a small target green with a collar, tall fescue along the west edge and the creek side, large broadleaf trees along the creek, a long grey metal shed (the same corrugated model as the pump house, solid) beside the lot, and white boundary stakes. Four red flags on tall poles (numbered 1-4, nearest first, no cup: the ball cannot be holed) to aim at, and six hitting spots (two are in bunkers); the ball is teed on grass spots. All four flags stay up; the Flag button picks which one the aim snaps to. After each shot the camera follows the ball, rests on where it stopped for about a second, then returns to the hitting spot.

EAGLE RIDGE SOUTH (eagle_south)
===============================

Early build: the OSM and lidar data only, nothing painted yet (no fescue, painted boundary, cart paths, tee boxes or site props).

Deep rough: dark green long rough (engine `longRough`), not the golden fescue of Woodbine Bend: the ground, the tall blades and the tufts in it are dark greens (`LONG_HSL`, `LONG_COLS`). It plays the same as any deep rough.

Wild turkeys (a flock of four on about 4 holes in 10, `scene.turkeys` = .4): the same flock and behaviour as at Woodbine Bend.

Gopher (`scene.groundhog`) and banner plane (`scene.bannerPlane`): as at Woodbine Bend.

No highway: US 20 runs about 2 km south of the course, off the map, so there is no traffic.

Side road (out of bounds, for looks only): Eagle Ridge Drive, the resort road past the clubhouse. No traffic.

Water: three ponds (OSM golf=water_hazard; the big one between holes 9 and 18) and the streams OSM maps through the valley (about 3.5 km in 10 pieces, from `creek.py osm`, which leaves out the stretches inside a pond), drawn as creek water. Pond shores are smooth curves (no lidar relief on the water or its banks, `RELIEF_OFF_WATER`).

Trees: about 9,900 from the lidar (6,100 broadleaf, 3,750 conifer, a few shrubs), the wooded valley the holes run through.

Buildings: the 10 OSM buildings on and beside the course, drawn as plain buildings. No clubhouse model, parked carts, cart ride, hot dog, fountain, signs or songs yet (none of those `scene` settings are set).

Practice green and driving range: Woodbine Bend's, as for every course.

STONEHENGE (stonehenge)
=======================

Listed as "Stonehenge"; records count from 2026-10-30. Early build: the OSM and lidar data only, nothing painted (no fescue, painted boundary, cart paths, tee boxes or site props). Blue tees played (6,350 yd, par 72); black, white and red are markers.

Deep rough: dark green long rough (engine `longRough`), as at Eagle Ridge. It plays the same as any deep rough.

White-tailed deer (`scene.deer` = .5: about half the holes have them; the others have none): a doe with a fawn (spotted, smaller) or a second doe, and on about a third of those holes a buck with antlers too.
  - Each appears 6-36 s after arriving on the hole, walks across the line of play, stops to graze with its head down for 3-9 s at a time, looks round as it walks, and leaves the far side; it is back 30-90 s later.
  - They bound away (10 m/s, white tail up) if the ball lands within ~18 m or flies within ~45 m, and walk again once the ball is 70 m off.
  - Can be hit (the body, not the legs): "Oh deer!", the ball ricochets.

Squirrels (`scene.squirrels` = 3 on every hole; grey ones and fox squirrels, a few times real size so they show):
  - Each appears 3-28 s after arriving, at a tree near the hole (within 30 m of the hole's play area), and dashes in bursts to another tree 8-45 m away, sitting up and flicking its tail between dashes; at the tree it runs up the trunk and is gone, back 10-40 s later.
  - Run for the nearest tree if the ball lands within ~9 m or flies within ~25 m.
  - Can be hit: "Nailed a squirrel!", with a squeak.
  - A hole with fewer than two trees near it has none.

No wild turkeys, gopher or banner plane.

IL 22 (the highway, with the traffic in part 1): runs past the south side of the course by the clubhouse drive, 50 mph all along (no slower stretch).

Side road (out of bounds, for looks only): Old Barrington Road past the northeast corner. No traffic.

Water: the OSM ponds (lateral and water hazards; water beside most holes, both sides of 18) and the OSM streams through the course (about 740 m in 3 pieces, from `creek.py osm`, clipped to the map), drawn as creek water. Pond shores are smooth curves (`RELIEF_OFF_WATER`).

No cart paths yet (OSM maps them, but the game takes cart paths only from `cartpaths_read.py`).

Trees: about 4,400: the 2007 Lake County lidar's trees, checked against the August 2023 aerial (`trees_aerial.py`, `TREES_GONE` in its course.py): about 420 lidar trees with no canopy on the aerial were taken out as felled since, 65 more on roofs and gravel, about 550 crowns widened to what the aerial shows and about 1,260 trees added where the aerial shows canopy nobody had placed (about 280 inside the course; most in the yards and woods round it).

Buildings: the OSM buildings on and beside the course (the clubhouse among them), drawn as plain buildings. No clubhouse model, parked carts, cart ride, hot dog, fountain, signs or songs.

Practice green and driving range: Woodbine Bend's, as for every course (the course's own range is not built).

Buildings: the OSM buildings on and beside the course (the clubhouse among them; the houses along 33rd Street and the north edge), drawn as plain buildings. No clubhouse model, parked carts, cart ride, hot dog, fountain, signs or songs.

Practice green and driving range: Woodbine Bend's, as for every course (the course's own range is not built).

CHESTNUT MOUNTAIN DISC GOLF (cmdg)
==================================

Listed as "CMDG (alpha)": a disc golf course (engine `disc`), in alpha (`alpha` in courses.json: no round is posted to the world leaderboard and no record cards). The 9 holes on the ski hill at Chestnut Mountain Resort, all par 3, played twice: holes 1-9 from the long tees (2,972 ft), 10-18 from the short ones.

The disc: thrown instead of a ball, drawn as a flat disc in the colour of the disc in hand (distance driver orange, fairway driver blue, midrange yellow, putter pink), tilted to its bank in flight and lying flat once down. No tee peg.

Baskets (one per hole, in place of the cup and flagstick; 1.3x real size): a steel pole, a yellow tray and top band, two rings of chains, and a small red hole flag on a mast above. A disc that flies into the chains slowly enough is caught (chain jingle); faster it rattles through and drops out; the tray rim, top band and pole knock it down (a clank).

Tee pads: a 1.6 x 3.6 m mat at each tee, square to the basket. No tee markers.

Fairways: the open, mown ground within 9 m of each hole's line (no canopy over 2 m in the lidar). Everything else is rough, and deep rough well away from play.

No wild turkeys, gopher or banner plane.

No highway. Side road (out of bounds, for looks only): West Chestnut Mountain Road, the resort road up to the lodge. No traffic.

Water: the OSM ponds by the lodge (the Mississippi's backwaters on the map's western edge are left off).

Trees: about 6,000 from the lidar (almost all broadleaf: oaks and hickories on the bluff; a few conifers).

Buildings: the 6 OSM buildings (the lodge and the resort's buildings), drawn as plain buildings. No ski lifts, alpine slide or other resort props yet.

Practice green and driving range: Woodbine Bend's, as for every course (with golf clubs).

ADLER PARK DISC GOLF (adgc)
===========================

Listed as "ADGC (Alpha)": a disc golf course (engine `disc`), in alpha (`alpha` in courses.json: no round is posted to the world leaderboard and no record cards). The 9 holes in Adler Memorial Park, Libertyville, all par 3 (2,740 ft), with one tee per hole: holes 10-18 are holes 1-9 again from the same tees.

The disc, baskets and tee pads: as at Chestnut Mountain (above).

Fairways: the open, mown ground within 9 m of each hole's line (no canopy over 2 m in the lidar). Everything else is rough, and deep rough well away from play.

No wild turkeys, gopher or banner plane.

No highway. Side roads (out of bounds, for looks only): North Milwaukee Avenue (IL 21) past the arts centre on the west, drawn as its two carriageways north of the arts centre and one road south of it, and West Adler Drive by the school. No traffic.

Water: the Des Plaines River past holes 8 and 9 (cut out of OSM's river area) and the OSM ponds nearby.

Trees: about 2,800 from the 2007 Lake County lidar (almost all broadleaf; a few conifers and shrubs). The lidar is old, so trees planted or felled since are not shown.

Buildings: the OSM buildings round the park (the rec centre, the pool house, the arts centre, the school and the houses beyond), drawn as plain buildings.

Adler Park Pool (between holes 4 and 5, `POOL` in its course.py): a pale concrete deck with four pools on it in white coping (the leisure pool, the lap pool joined to it with 8 lane lines, a side pool and a kiddie pool), a blue water slide (a steel tower with stairs and a tube spiralling down to the kiddie pool), six teal shade canopies on posts, and a 1.8 m chain-link fence round the lot (OSM's pool outline). The fenced lot is out of bounds (a hole in the course boundary; the fence is the line, no stakes). The slide tower is solid ("Off the water slide!"); the fence and canopies are not. No long grass grows on the deck.

Playground (by basket 3, `PLAYGROUND` in its course.py): a wood-chip mulch bed in a timber edging; a play structure of two decks on green posts with red and blue pyramid roofs, a railed bridge between them, a yellow slide and a red one, a ladder; a blue swing set with three swings out front. The two decks are solid ("Off the playground!").

Practice green and driving range: Woodbine Bend's, as for every course (with golf clubs).
